01  /  About

An aspiring game developer with a builder's mindset.

I'm passionate about creating immersive, gameplay-focused experiences and writing clean, efficient, maintainable code. I've spent years working in teams on story- and gameplay-driven games — an enthusiastic, hard-working team player who enjoys solving hard problems.

My journey started in 2020 with my first game-development course. Since then I've shipped personal and team projects, and in 2023 I enrolled at Saxion University of Applied Sciences for a Bachelor's in Creative Media & Game Technologies.

During my studies I spent five months interning at Artking Studios as a game developer, working across both code and UI on a racing-game vertical slice in Unity — hands-on production experience that sharpened how I build real, shippable features.
